01 · Where we're starting

Your current logo

It has a lot of good ideas. The redesign keeps the meaning and makes it simpler, so it stays clear at every size, from a van door to a phone icon.

✓Keep

  • Teal + gold: calm, medical and premium
  • Stethoscope and heartbeat say "real clinical care"
  • A warm, personal feel from the script lettering
  • The name "Trusted" is a strong promise

!Improve

  • Too many separate pieces, so the eye has nowhere to land
  • Shiny 3D/metal effects blur when small and won't embroider or print in one colour
  • Script "Trusted" is hard to read on a phone or favicon
  • No icon that works alone (app icon, profile picture)
  • Nothing says "we come to your home" or "telehealth"

04 · Website plan

What we learned from the reference site

We reviewed morganfamhcs.com, a mobile healthcare practice in San Antonio, TX, and noted what it does well and what's missing.

✓What it does well

  • Phone and email in a top bar on every page
  • A clear "Set an Appointment" button
  • Services shown as tiles: in-home primary care, telemedicine, mobile IV, mobile X-ray & lab, wound care, weight management, women's health
  • A "Send your referrals" page for doctors and partners
  • Mission statement, service area and Careers page
  • Translation menu for other languages

!What we'll do better

  • Telehealth is only a page there; we'll add a "Request a telehealth visit" form in a couple of clicks
  • A secure message form instead of just an email link
  • A "Do you serve my area?" ZIP code check
  • Insurance / self-pay prices and an FAQ
  • Patient reviews and a "Meet your provider" section to build trust
  • A sticky "Call / Book" bar on phones, where most patients will visit
