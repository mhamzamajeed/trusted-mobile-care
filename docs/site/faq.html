Frequently asked questions

How soon can I be seen?

Most home visits are booked within the same week, and telehealth visits usually within 24–48 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We visit Your City & surrounding areas. Use the ZIP check on the booking page or call us.

Is telehealth as good as an in-person visit?

For many common problems, yes. If your provider decides you need to be seen in person, we can book a home visit.

What should I have ready for a home visit?

Your ID, insurance card, a list of your medications, and a quiet, well-lit space.

Can you send prescriptions to my pharmacy?

Yes. Prescriptions are sent electronically to the pharmacy you choose.

Is my information private?

Yes. We follow HIPAA privacy rules. Please don't send detailed medical information through the general message form.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Our services are not for emergencies.
