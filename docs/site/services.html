Our services

Real medical care, where you are.

Every service can be booked as a home visit, and many can start with a telehealth visit.

In-home primary care

Adults & seniorsSame-week visits

Checkups, sick visits and follow-ups at your door.

Who it's for: Anyone who finds it hard to get to a clinic: seniors, busy parents, people recovering at home.

What to expect

  • Full exam and vital signs
  • Treatment for common illnesses
  • Prescriptions and refills
  • Coordination with your specialists

Telehealth visits

From anywhereVideo or phone

See a provider by secure video or phone.

Who it's for: Minor illnesses, medication questions, follow-ups and lab result reviews.

What to expect

  • Secure video link sent by text
  • Visit usually within 24–48 hours
  • E-prescriptions to your pharmacy
  • Home visit booked if needed

Mobile IV therapy

HydrationVitamins

Hydration and vitamin infusions at home.

Who it's for: Dehydration, recovery after illness, fatigue, or wellness support as advised by your provider.

What to expect

  • Short health screening first
  • Licensed nurse starts and monitors the IV
  • Usually 45–60 minutes
  • Aftercare instructions
Request this service

Mobile labs

No waiting roomResults reviewed

Blood draws and specimen collection at home.

Who it's for: Routine labs, ordered tests from your doctor, and monitoring for chronic conditions.

What to expect

  • Bring your lab order or we can order
  • Collection at home, sent to the lab
  • Results reviewed with you
  • Copies sent to your doctor

Wound care

Post-surgeryChronic wounds

Dressing changes, checks and healing plans.

Who it's for: Surgical wounds, pressure sores, diabetic wounds and slow-healing injuries.

What to expect

  • Wound assessment and measurement
  • Cleaning and dressing changes
  • Infection checks
  • Healing progress notes for your doctor
